Industrial network configuration

The 1783-BMS20CL is a 20-port Stratix 5700 managed switch with 16 fixed RJ45 ports, two SFP ports and two RJ45/SFP combo ports. It is designed for DIN-rail installation in industrial control and automation networks, with dual DC power inputs and an alarm relay connection.

Condition & verification

Clean used condition with the Pwr A, Pwr B and alarm terminal blocks fitted.

Tested at approximately 24 V DC with normal startup, and Rockwell Device Manager was accessed successfully. All 18 RJ45 copper interfaces passed link and data-communication testing: ports 1–16 plus the RJ45 sides of combo ports 19–20.

The switch was returned to factory defaults after testing. The SFP interfaces were not exercised.
