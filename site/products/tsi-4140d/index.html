Condition & verification

Open-box, very clean laboratory surplus. The instrument, display, housing, original fitted packaging and documentation present well. Model, serial number, included components and physical condition have been verified.

Powered and functionally tested using a compatible 7.5 V DC centre-positive supply. The instrument indicated approximately 0.009 Std L/min with no intentional flow and responded to low-pressure clean-air flow below 5 psi, indicating approximately 2.67 Std L/min during the audit test.

This was a functional response test. The matching TSI factory calibration certificate is dated 8 April 2014.
