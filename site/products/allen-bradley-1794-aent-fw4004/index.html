FLEX I/O network application

The 1794-AENT is the EtherNet/IP communication adapter for an Allen-Bradley FLEX I/O station. It connects digital, analogue and other FLEX I/O modules to an industrial EtherNet/IP network in Rockwell PLC and remote-I/O systems.

Condition & verification

Clean used industrial condition. The external housings, address thumbwheels and fitted power-terminal hardware are complete, with no material housing damage identified in the saleable group photographs.

Each adapter was factory reset, powered at 24 V DC and accessed through its built-in Rockwell web diagnostics over Ethernet. The representative diagnostic capture reports 1794-AENT/B identity and product revision 4.004 Build 2.

This verification confirms powered startup and TCP/IP access to the adapter diagnostics; no commissioned PLC I/O session was performed.
