OEM gas-handling configuration

The Thomas 118ZC20/24UV is a compact 24 V BLDC diaphragm pump for pressure and vacuum duties in precision gas-handling, sampling and instrumentation systems. Thomas specifies electronic motor control for this platform; the matching SK604530 controller and complete interconnecting harness are included here as one tested assembly.

Condition & verification

Very clean used condition. The pump, controller and wiring present well, with protective red port caps fitted.

Tested together at 24 V DC and operating normally. The pump starts at approximately 3 A and settles at approximately 1.5 A running unloaded.
